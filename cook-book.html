Cook Book

An accessible cookbook website designed with visually impaired users in mind, with clear recipe structure and multiple ways to access instructions.

Role UX designerDuration June–August 2024Focus Screen readers · Text-to-speech · Clear navigation

The story

Problem

Dense recipe websites can make information difficult to access and cooking steps hard to follow.

Goal

Create a navigable cookbook experience that supports different ways of accessing recipe information.

Accessibility by design

Screen-reader compatibility, text-to-speech, audio descriptions, alt text, and clear navigation are treated as core features.
