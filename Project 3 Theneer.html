UI/UX Case Study

Theneer — Coffee & Snack Ordering App

Theneer is a mobile application designed to simplify the process of ordering coffee and healthy snacks for busy professionals and students. The app offers customization options, including decaffeinated coffee, and enhances efficiency with features like pre-ordering and self-pickup.

Role UX/UI Designer
Duration Aug – Nov 2023
Timeline 3 Months
Problem

Ordering coffee shouldn't be this hard

Busy professionals and students frequently struggle to order coffee and nutritious snacks due to long wait times, a lack of healthy selections, and the difficulty of finding nearby cafes that meet their dietary preferences.

Goal

Make ordering simple and personal

The app aims to simplify the ordering process and offer personalized options to improve user satisfaction and efficiency.

Research

Understanding the target audience

Conducted interviews with the target audience to understand their pain points. We found that they struggle to maintain a healthy lifestyle amid their busy schedules. We focused on these pain points and frustrations to ideate and develop a final product. Performed a competitive analysis of existing coffee ordering applications to understand their strengths and weaknesses — this analysis helped identify opportunities for differentiation and improvement.

My Role

UX/UI Designer

Responsibilities spanned user research, wireframing, prototyping, usability testing, iteration, and high-fidelity prototyping.

User Insight

Pain Points

Limited options for decaffeinated coffee at nearby cafes.

Difficulty finding healthy snacks that provide sustained energy.

Stress and frustration from waiting in long queues in crowded cafes.

No dedicated, purpose-built coffee ordering app existed for this audience.

Persona & Problem Statement

Meet Manikandan

M

Manikandan, a medical student, needs decaffeinated coffee due to his overconsumption of caffeine to stay alert, but struggles to find such options in nearby cafes.

Competitive Analysis

Finding the gap in the market

Evaluated existing coffee apps, identifying gaps in customization, pre-ordering features, and healthy snack availability. This guided the design differentiation for Theneer.

Ideation & User Journey Map

Designing around Ashley's wait-time anxiety

Focused on users like Ashley, who value features that minimize wait times. The goal was to streamline the ordering process with pre-order and pickup options.

Paper Wireframes

Sketching the core screens

Developed multiple versions of the app's key screens, iterating on options for order customization and delivery visibility. The wireframes focused on simplicity and ease of navigation.

Design — Digital Wireframes

From sketches to structured screens

Created digital wireframes that incorporated two essential features:

Order Customization — allowing users to easily personalize their coffee and snack orders.

Pre-Ordering & Self-Pickup — emphasized to reduce waiting times and stress.

User Testing & Usability Study

Simplifying an overwhelming interface

Users initially felt overwhelmed by the customization options. In response, I simplified the customization interface by highlighting the most frequently selected options.

Mockups

Iterating from feedback

Based on usability testing feedback, the customization process was revised to enhance user clarity. Additional features like nutritional facts were added to support users' health-conscious decisions.

Before
After
High-Fidelity Prototype

The final, polished flow

The high-fidelity design was updated based on feedback from the usability study. To address users feeling overwhelmed during customization, frequently selected options were highlighted to simplify decision-making, and nutritional facts were added to support health-conscious choices.

Accessibility Considerations

Designing for every user

Included compatibility with screen readers and voice control.

Implemented local language options to make the app accessible to non-English users.

Reflection

What I Learned

Impact: Designing Theneer underscored the importance of prioritizing user experience by addressing pain points directly through thoughtful design. By focusing on simplicity, customization, and accessibility, the app successfully meets the needs of busy professionals and students.

What I learned: I learned the importance of being an advocate for the user. Every design decision should be grounded in what will best serve the users, making their experience as smooth and enjoyable as possible.

Next Steps

Going forward

Continue refining the ordering flow based on usability findings, improving navigation clarity and streamlining pre-ordering and pickup even further.

Incorporate additional accessibility features, including improved keyboard navigation, stronger screen reader support, and customizable interface options.
