LifeLink AI

An emergency medical response and ambulance dispatch system concept built around clearer decisions during a hospital handoff.

Role Product designer Duration Codathon 2026 · Team of 4 Focus Patient information · Vitals · AI analysis · Hospital matching

The story

Problem

Emergency teams need a focused way to move from patient information and vital signs to analysis, destination selection, and hospital notification without losing context.

Design goal

Make high-priority information visible and the next action clear without making the workflow overwhelming.

Prototype flow

The prototype includes patient inputs, vital monitoring, injury image upload, AI analysis, hospital recommendations, direct ER call, patient summary, and alert confirmation.

Technology

Built with Flask, SQLite, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript by a team of four. This is a UX/UI product concept and prototype, not a clinically validated medical product.
