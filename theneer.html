Theneer

A mobile application designed to simplify ordering coffee and healthy snacks for busy professionals and students.

Role UX/UI designer Duration August–November 2023 Focus Coffee ordering · Customization · Accessibility

The story

Problem

Busy professionals and students struggle with long waits, limited healthy selections, and finding cafes that meet their dietary preferences.

Goal

Simplify ordering and offer personalized options that improve confidence, satisfaction, and efficiency.

Research & insights

People wanted sustained energy, less time in queues, and clearer ways to find and customize suitable drinks and snacks.
