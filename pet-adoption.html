Pet adoption

A user-friendly platform that simplifies pet adoption, connects people with shelters, supports visit scheduling, and offers online veterinary advice.

Role UX/UI designer Duration February–May 2024 Focus Pet browsing · Shelter visits · Medical assistance

The story

Problem

Limited time with pets, unclear visiting hours, confusing scheduling, and difficulty accessing veterinary advice make adoption harder.

Goal

Make adoption more transparent by bringing pet information, scheduling, appointments, and medical support together.

Design decisions

Clear pet profiles, visit scheduling, appointment booking, and a dedicated medical assistance path make the next step easier to understand.
